# Foodcom-Recipe-Popularity-Analysis
This project analyzes 500K+ Food.com recipes and 1.4M reviews to uncover what makes dishes popular and satisfying. It reveals that simplicity, balance, and sensory appeal drive higher user ratings and engagement.

Executive Summary：

1.Objectives and Methods

Analyzed 500K+ Food.com recipes and 1.4M reviews to uncover what drives recipe popularity and satisfaction.

Merged and cleaned large datasets, then explored how ingredients, nutrition, and cooking time affect ratings and visibility using EDA and visualization in Python.

Key Findings：

1.Popularity ≠ Satisfaction: More reviews don’t always mean higher ratings.

2.Simplicity Wins: Recipes with 10–14 ingredients rate highest.

3.Balanced Nutrition: Moderate calories and nutrients yield better satisfaction.

4.Quality Over Speed: Users value taste and outcome more than quick prep time.

5.Language Matters: Sensory words like crispy or garlic boost engagement.

Conclusion：

1.Successful recipes balance taste, health, and effort—familiar, practical, and rewarding.

2.These insights guide food platforms, creators, and brands toward data-driven design of engaging, satisfying dishes.
